# 03 — Problem Framing

## Problem statement

Traditional physical attendance methods in SENA technical and technological classrooms
cause substantial pedagogical time loss, vulnerability to fraud, and bureaucratic overhead.

## Pain points analysis (AS-IS)

| Symptom | Classroom reality | Business impact |
|---|---|---|
| Pedagogical time loss | Verbal roll calls take 15 to 25 minutes per 6-hour session | ~6.6 hours of training time lost monthly per ficha |
| Proxy attendance fraud | Absent students ask classmates to sign physical paper sheets | Compromised legal integrity of public training records |
| Binary attendance unfairness | Students 20 min late due to transit receive 6h absence (0h awarded) | Student demoralization and increased dropout rates |
| Bureaucratic transcription | Instructors transcribe paper sheets to Excel or Sofia Plus | Delayed reporting and manual clerical error risks |

## Root causes

1. Reliance on physical paper sign-in sheets.
2. Lack of automated time calculation distinguishing punctual presence from partial tardiness.
3. Absence of mobile-first verification tools tailored to heterogeneous student devices.
