## Product summary

- **Initiative:** Classroom Attendance Digitalization and Fraud Mitigation.
- **Target Organization:** SENA (Centro de Formación / ADSO).
- **Core Value Metric:** Reduce roll-call overhead from 25 min to < 1 min per apprentice.
- **Inclusion Principle:** Zero exclusion of apprentices due to device or network constraints.

## Success criteria

1. Punctuality computed fairly and proportionally rather than binary all-or-nothing.
2. Attendance verified without reliance on expensive or intrusive biometrics.
3. Administrative compliance automated with export-ready sheets.
